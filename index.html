Mad Molecool

♛ Best AI/ML Hack
♛ Best Use of Intel® Developer Cloud

An electronic lab notebook for molecular biologists, with a fine-tuned LLM lab partner built around the Design-Build-Test-Learn workflow. It summarizes experiments with the Gemini API and keeps a chemical encyclopedia close by.

Fine-tuned on web-scraped journal articles plus PubMedQA and BioASQ, then shrunk with BF16, INT8 quantization and OpenVINO so it runs fast on Intel CPUs.

✦ $25K Best AI for Good grant
✦ Runner-up, Skydeck $25K Grand Prize

A platform for learning American Sign Language with real-time feedback. The camera tracks your hands with pose estimation, and AI-driven lessons tell you what to fix.

Frontend in Next.js and Tailwind. Models trained with PyTorch, TensorFlow and Keras on Intel Tiber Developer Cloud, with Google Search and OpenAI APIs powering grounded RAG answers.

Watchdog

♛ 1st place, Hyperbolic track · $1,000

An AI school-safety system that watches live security footage for weapons. When it finds one, it alerts the whole building automatically and describes the suspect for responders.

A YOLOv8 detector trained on Hyperbolic GPUs, Llama 3.2 Vision for suspect descriptions, audio threat detection, Deepgram for talking to responders, a Convex dashboard with an interactive floor plan, and MMS alerts from Python.
